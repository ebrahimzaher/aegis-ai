Why was I charged twice for my subscription?

Duplicate charges usually happen when a payment retry occurs after a temporary bank decline that was later approved. Both charges will show in your billing history, but only one is a valid charge. Duplicate charges are automatically refunded within 5-7 business days. If you don't see the refund after 7 business days, contact support with your order ID.

How do I update my payment method?

Go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods, then click "Add new card". Once added, set it as default. Your next billing cycle will use the new payment method automatically.

When does my subscription renew?

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same day each billing period (monthly or annually), based on your original signup date. You can view your exact renewal date under Account Settings > Subscription.

Can I get an invoice for my payment?

Yes. All invoices are available under Account Settings > Billing > Invoices, and are also emailed automatically after every successful payment.
