What is the refund window?

Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of the original purchase date, no questions asked. After 14 days, refunds are evaluated case by case.

How long does a refund take to process?

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Bank processing times may add a few extra days before it appears on your statement.

Are annual subscriptions refundable?

Annual subscriptions are refundable in full within the first 14 days. After that, we offer a prorated refund for unused months only if the cancellation is due to a service issue on our side.

Do refunds require manager approval?

Refunds under $100 can be auto-approved by the support agent. Refunds of $100 or more, or any refund requested after the 14-day window, require manager approval before being issued.
