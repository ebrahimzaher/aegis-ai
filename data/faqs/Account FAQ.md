I can't log in to my account, what should I do?

First try resetting your password from the login page using "Forgot password". If you still can't log in after resetting, your account may be temporarily locked after multiple failed attempts — this unlocks automatically after 30 minutes.

How do I change the email linked to my account?

Go to Account Settings > Profile > Email, enter the new email, and confirm via the verification link sent to the new address. The old email remains active until the new one is verified.

How do I delete my account?

Account deletion requests must be submitted through Account Settings

Privacy > Delete Account. This is irreversible and removes all associated data within 30 days, in line with data retention policy.

Can I have two accounts with the same email?

No, each email address can only be linked to one account. If you need separate accounts, each must use a unique email address.
